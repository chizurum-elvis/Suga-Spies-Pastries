# Menu ingredient research for owner review

## Purpose and safety boundary

This document gives the owner a researched starting checklist for the ten launch products. It does **not** state the actual Suga & Spies recipe and must not be copied into the customer-facing catalogue yet.

An accurate ingredient statement can only come from the recipe actually used, including every brand and all component ingredients in items such as chocolate, margarine, bouillon, sausage meat, and prepared pastry. The Canadian Food Inspection Agency says priority allergens must be declared even when they occur inside another ingredient, and precautionary “May contain” wording must reflect real cross-contact controls rather than a guess: [CFIA food allergen labelling guidance](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/food-allergen-labelling) and [CFIA allergen-labelling tips](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/allergen-labelling).

The website therefore keeps `ingredients` and `allergen_information` blank until the owner completes the verification steps below.

## Researched working ingredient checklists

These are representative recipe bases from established baking sources. “Confirm” means the owner must replace the working list with the exact Suga & Spies formulation.

### 1. 9" Cheesecake

Working base: graham cracker crumbs, confectioners’ sugar, butter, salt, cream cheese, granulated sugar, eggs, and vanilla.

Confirm: exact crust, flavour/topping, sour cream or lemon if used, and all packaged component ingredients.

Source: [King Arthur Baking — Easy Cheesecake](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/easy-cheesecake-recipe?page=0).

### 2. 2" Mini Cheesecake

Working base: graham cracker crumbs, butter, sugar, salt, cream cheese, vanilla, sour cream, eggs, and lemon juice.

Confirm: whether the mini recipe differs from the full cheesecake and every approved topping or flavour.

Source: [King Arthur Baking — Mini Cheesecakes](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/mini-cheesecakes-recipe).

### 3. Chocolate Chip/Chunk Cookie

Working base: wheat flour, brown sugar, granulated sugar, butter or other recipe fat, salt, vanilla, leavener, egg, and semisweet chocolate chips and/or chunks.

Confirm: exact fat, leavener, chocolate brand and type, and whether vanilla, vinegar, or another flavouring is used. Chocolate can contain additional milk or soy ingredients that must be captured from its package label.

Source: [King Arthur Baking — Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/classic-chocolate-chip-cookies-recipe).

### 4. Red Velvet Cookie

Working base: wheat flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, salt, butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar, egg, milk or buttermilk, vanilla, red food colouring, and chocolate chips if used.

Confirm: the exact colouring, whether chocolate pieces or cream cheese are present, and the component ingredients of each packaged addition. The menu name alone does not confirm white chocolate.

Sources: [Sally’s Baking — Red Velvet Chocolate Chip Cookies](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/red-velvet-chocolate-chip-cookies/) and [Good Food — Red Velvet Cookies](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/red-velvet-cookies).

### 5. Scone

Working base: wheat flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, butter, eggs, vanilla, and milk, half-and-half, or cream.

Confirm: the actual liquid and fat, whether the launch scone is plain, and every fruit, chocolate, nut, spice, or topping used.

Source: [King Arthur Baking — Scones](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/scones-recipe).

### 6. Muffin

Working base: wheat flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, butter or vegetable oil, sugar, eggs, vanilla, and sour cream or yogurt.

Confirm: the launch muffin flavour and its add-ins. The current stock photo shows blueberries, but a photograph is not recipe confirmation.

Source: [King Arthur Baking — Blueberry Muffins](https://cmsstage.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/blueberry-muffins-recipe).

### 7. 9" Tart

Working pastry base: butter, sugar, salt, egg yolk, vanilla, water or milk, and wheat flour.

Confirm: the tart filling, glaze, fruit or garnish, and whether the business uses this style of sweet pastry. The current photograph must not be used as proof that the product is a fruit tart.

Source: [King Arthur Baking — Pâte Sucrée](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/pate-sucree-recipe).

### 8. Meat Pie

Working pastry base: wheat flour, margarine or butter, baking powder, salt, and water. Representative filling: ground beef, onion, carrots, potatoes, oil, seasoning or bouillon, thyme, curry, flour as a binder, water, and salt.

Confirm: exact meat, vegetables, fat, seasonings, bouillon brand, binder, egg wash, and whether this representative Nigerian-style preparation matches the business recipe. Bouillon and seasoning blends may contain additional allergens.

Source: [Nigerian Food TV — Nigerian Meat Pie](https://www.nigerianfoodtv.com/how-to-make-nigerian-meat-pie/).

### 9. Chicken Pie

Working pastry base: wheat flour, baking powder, salt, margarine or butter, and water. Representative filling: chicken, onion, optional garlic, carrots, potatoes, oil, seasoning or bouillon, thyme, curry, flour as a binder, water, and salt; egg may be used to seal or finish the pastry.

Confirm: exact chicken cut, vegetables, fat, seasonings, bouillon brand, binder, and egg wash.

Source: [Nigerian Food TV — Nigerian Chicken Pie](https://www.nigerianfoodtv.com/how-to-make-nigerian-chicken-pie/).

### 10. Sausage Roll

Working base: sausage meat, pastry, egg wash, and possible breadcrumbs, herbs, onion, flour, or seeds.

Confirm: sausage brand and full component list, pastry recipe or packaged label, seasonings, glaze, and toppings. Sausage and prepared pastry can contain allergens that are not obvious from the product name.

Source: [Good Food — Sausage Rolls](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/sausage-rolls).

## Owner verification required before publication

For each product, the owner must provide:

1. The exact production recipe, including optional toppings and every allowed flavour.
2. A current label or supplier specification for every packaged ingredient.
3. The ingredient list in descending order by weight if Canadian rules require it for the way the product is sold.
4. Confirmed `Contains` allergens using Canadian prescribed source names.
5. A cross-contact assessment for the actual kitchen, equipment, utensils, storage, and suppliers before any `May contain` statement is approved.
6. The date and name of the person who approved the final customer-facing wording.

Likely allergens across these representative recipes include wheat, milk, and egg; soy, sesame, mustard, sulphites, peanuts, or tree nuts may also arise from particular chocolate, sausage, seasoning, pastry, toppings, or shared equipment. These are risk prompts—not final declarations.
